### CHEM-000235: Generic coupled-cluster singles-and-doubles root counts
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Derive a usable formula for the generic number of isolated complex CCSD amplitude solutions as electron and orbital counts vary, distinguishing arbitrary algebraic systems from physical two-body Hamiltonians.
- **Status:** Algebraic geometry provides bounds and special cases. The Grassmannian CCS degree is solved; arbitrary-size CCSD root counts are not.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Quantifies solution multiplicity and the completeness requirements of continuation algorithms.
- **Key works:** F. M. Faulstich, B. Sturmfels and S. Sverrisdóttir (2025), *Algebraic Varieties in Quantum Chemistry*, [DOI:10.1007/s10208-024-09657-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10208-024-09657-8).
- **Related:** Algebraic geometry: degrees of truncated exponential varieties; numerical algebraic geometry: complete homotopy tracking.
